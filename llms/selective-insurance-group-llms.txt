# Selective Insurance Group

> Selective Insurance Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: SIGI) is a Branchville, New Jersey insurance
> holding company. Its subsidiaries underwrite standard commercial lines, standard personal
> lines, and excess and surplus lines property and casualty insurance, and write flood
> insurance as a Write Your Own carrier for the National Flood Insurance Program. All
> business is distributed through independent agents and brokers.

## Important note for agents

Selective Insurance Group publishes **no public developer program, no API documentation, and
no machine-readable API contract**. As of 2026-08-28 the following were probed and none
returned a contract: `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/llms.txt`, `/developers`,
`/developer`, `/api`, `developer.selective.com`, `api.selective.com`, and every
`/.well-known/*` discovery path on `www.selective.com`,
`customer1.selectiveinsurance.com` and `agent.selectiveflood.com`.

Machine integration with Selective happens through the independent-agency channel — policy,
billing and claims data reaches an agency's management system by download, and agents work in
Selective's own login-gated portal. There is no self-service, publicly documented API an agent
or developer can call.
